LBAudio plugins: installing on Windows
======================================

1. Copy each .vst3 folder from this zip into:

       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\

   (Copy the whole .vst3 folder, not just what's inside it. Windows may ask
   for administrator permission: click Continue.)

2. Open your DAW and rescan plugins. They appear under "LBAudio".

Works in any 64-bit Windows DAW that supports VST3: Ableton Live, FL Studio,
Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig and others.
