LBAudio plugins: installing on Linux
====================================

1. Copy each .vst3 folder from this zip into ~/.vst3/
   (create the folder if it doesn't exist):

       mkdir -p ~/.vst3 && cp -r *.vst3 ~/.vst3/

2. Open your DAW (Reaper, Bitwig, Ardour and others) and rescan plugins.
   They appear under "LBAudio".
