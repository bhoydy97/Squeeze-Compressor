LBAudio plugins: installing on a Mac
====================================

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 10.13 or later.

1. Copy each .vst3 into:          ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   and each .component into:      ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/

   To get there: in Finder choose Go > Go to Folder..., paste the path,
   and press Return. Create the folder if it isn't there.

   Use the .vst3 for Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig and
   FL Studio; the .component (Audio Unit) for Logic Pro and GarageBand.

2. Open your DAW and rescan plugins. They appear under "LBAudio".

If your DAW says a plugin "can't be opened" or is damaged, macOS has
quarantined the download. Open Terminal and run:

    xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

then restart your DAW.
