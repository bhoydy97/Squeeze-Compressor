What do I download?

One zip for your system with the FX 5 inside, plus INSTALL.txt with the steps. Windows, Mac and Linux zips are all included with your purchase.

How do I install them?

Copy each .vst3 into your VST3 folder: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 on Windows or ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 on a Mac. Logic users also copy each .component into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components. Then rescan plugins in your DAW.

How many computers can I use them on?

All the computers you personally own or use. See the licence agreement for the details.

Do updates cost anything?

No. Updates to version 1 of every plugin in the bundle are free.

What if one doesn't work on my system?

Get in touch within 14 days of buying. If we can't get it working, you get a full refund. See the refund policy.

Do they work in Pro Tools?

Not yet. Pro Tools only loads AAX plugins, and LBAudio plugins are VST3 and AU.
