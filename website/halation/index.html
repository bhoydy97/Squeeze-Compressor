Watch it work

The real plugin on screen, on one of our tracks. Every before-and-after is matched in loudness, so you hear what Halation does, not a change in volume.

Four bands, each in its own colour of light

The spectrum shows the four bands, how far each one is being turned down, and the three split points. Drag a split to move the bands.

A compressor in every band

A boomy bass note turns down only the bass; the vocal and the cymbals don't duck with it. Each band has its own threshold, ratio, attack, release and makeup.

An exciter in every band, in five tones

Excite adds harmonics made from that band alone, in the tone you pick: Warm, Tape, Bright, Crunch or Octave. Amount sets how much, and Mix blends it in. Octave gives a bass weight you can hear on a phone; Bright adds air on top.

Presets to start from

Factory presets for the mix bus, beats, vocals, instruments and mastering, plus Save for your own. Step through them with the arrows while the track plays.

Lit up, so you can read it

Halation is named after the glow around a bright light on film, and the window works the same way. Each band glows in its own colour, red for the lows through amber and green to violet for the highs, and a band you bypass goes dark.

The controls

Each band has its own Threshold, Ratio, Attack, Release and Makeup, an exciter with its own Tone, Amount and Mix, and Solo and Bypass; the splits, Input, Output and Mix are for the whole plugin. Double-click any knob to reset it, or click a value and type one in.

Threshold−60 to 0 dBThe level above which that band is turned down. A 6 dB soft knee keeps it smooth.

Attack0.1 to 200 msHow fast it reacts. Slower lets the front of each hit through.
Release5 ms to 2 sHow fast it lets go.
Makeup−12 to +24 dBThe band's level afterwards: use it to rebalance the bands as well as to make up.
Excite ToneWarm, Tape, Bright, Crunch, OctaveThe kind of harmonics: Warm is full and round, Tape soft and smooth, Bright crisp and airy, Crunch edgy and dense, and Octave adds the octave above the note. Changing it while the track plays doesn't click.
Excite Amount0 to 100 %How much the exciter adds, made from that band only. Turned up, the harmonics get denser as well as louder. It follows the band's level, so quiet parts get the same colour as loud ones.
Excite Mix0 to 100 %Blends the exciter in. Drive Amount hard for a dense colour, then bring Mix down to tuck it under the band.
Solo / BypassOn / offSolo to hear one band (or several) on its own. Bypass leaves a band untouched.
Input / Output−24 to +24 dBThe level into and out of Halation.
Mix0 to 100 %Blends in the original. It's phase-matched to the bands, so any blend stays clean.

Where to start

Four starting points. Set these, then adjust by ear.

  • Mix bus glue

    Preset Mix glue · or every band 2:1, thresholds set so each shows 1–3 dB

    Holds a mix together without the whole thing pumping on every kick.

  • 808 that cuts through

    Preset 808 weight · Low split 80–110 Hz · Low excite Octave, 50–70 %

    The sub stays steady, and the octave above it carries it on small speakers.

  • Harsh vocal, smoothed

    Preset Smooth vocal · High Mid 3:1, fast attack, splits around 2.5 and 8 kHz

    Turns the harsh notes down only when they happen, which an EQ cut can't do.

  • Master with air

    Preset Master polish · 1.5:1 everywhere · High excite Bright, 15–25 %

    Gentle control across the range, and a little extra sheen on top.

How do I install it?

Unzip the download and copy Halation.vst3 into your VST3 folder: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 on Windows or ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 on a Mac. Logic users also copy Halation.component into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components. Then rescan plugins in your DAW. The zip includes these steps in INSTALL.txt.

Where are my presets saved?

In an LBAudio/Halation/Presets folder: in Application Support in your user Library on a Mac, in AppData\Roaming on Windows and in ~/.config on Linux. Choose "Open your presets folder" in the preset menu to go straight there.

How is Excite different from an EQ boost?

An EQ boost turns up what's already there. Excite adds new harmonics above the notes in that band, so a dull or quiet part gains brightness or weight that an EQ can't find.

Which Excite tone should I use?

Start with Warm, then try the others by ear. Octave helps a bass or 808 come through on phones and laptops, Bright adds air to the top, Tape warms the lows and mids without an edge, and Crunch gives drums and rap vocals some bite.
